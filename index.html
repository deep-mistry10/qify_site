QR codes, explained

Useful information about the QR codes you can create with QIFY.

What is a QR code?

A QR code is a two-dimensional code that stores information such as a website address, text or contact details. A phone camera or QR scanner can read the information quickly.

What is a UPI QR code?

A UPI QR code contains payment information that a compatible UPI app can read. QIFY can create a UPI payment QR with a UPI ID, name and optional amount.

Static vs dynamic QR codes

A static QR code contains its destination or information directly. A dynamic QR system normally uses a redirect or service that can change the destination later. QIFY creates QR codes directly in your browser.

Do I need a UPI PIN to receive money?

You should never need to enter your UPI PIN simply to receive money. A UPI PIN is used to authorize payments from your account. Never share your PIN with another person.

Is my QR data uploaded?

This generator creates QR data in your browser. The QR generator itself does not send the text, URL, WiFi details or UPI details to a QIFY server for generation.

What can I create?

You can create QR codes for URLs, plain text, mobile numbers, email addresses, contacts, WiFi networks, UPI payments and WhatsApp chats.
