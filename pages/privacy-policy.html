Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Welcome to QIFY! We believe privacy policies shouldn't require a law degree to understand. This page explains how we handle your information when you visit and use our website and tools.

QIFY is an independent passion project built by Deep Mistry. By using the site, you agree to the simple rules below. If you aren't comfortable with these practices, that's entirely okay, but we ask that you don't use the website.

1. Information You Type Into QIFY

When you use our QR Code Generator (whether it's for a website, phone number, WiFi, UPI payment, or WhatsApp message), the magic happens right in your browser. You don't need to create an account, and we don't send the data you type to our servers just to make the code.

Heads up: Anyone who scans your generated QR code can read whatever information you put into it. Please be smart and avoid encoding sensitive secrets that you don't want the world to see!

2. What Goes Into Your QR Codes

Our tool makes different types of QR codes depending on what you need. It might hold a simple website link, your contact details, a pre-filled WhatsApp message, or your WiFi password.

Since you are the one supplying this information, please double-check your generated QR code to make sure it's exactly what you want before you print or share it.

3. What We DON'T Collect

We don't want to hoard your personal data. You don't have to register to make a QR code, and we don't save the text you type into the generator to any QIFY database.

That being said, the basic background technology that keeps the website secure and running does process some standard technical info (more on that below).

4. The Technical Stuff We Do Collect

Like almost every website on the internet, our servers and hosting providers automatically collect basic technical logs when you visit. This includes things like your IP address, browser type, what time you visited, and roughly where you are in the world.

This is strictly used to keep the site running smoothly. We never use this information to try and figure out who you are based on the QR codes you generate.

5. Cookies (Not the Chocolate Chip Kind)

We use cookies—tiny text files saved on your browser—to keep the site working, secure, and to show ads. Cookies help with things like remembering your preferences and measuring how people use the site.

You are always in control. If you don't like cookies, you can easily block or delete them using your web browser's settings.

6. Ads by Google

To keep QIFY free, we show ads using Google AdSense and other partners.

Google uses cookies to show you ads based on websites you've visited in the past. We don't hand-pick the individual ads you see, and they may be personalized based on your browsing habits.

If you want to opt out of personalized ads, you can easily do that in your Google Ads Settings. For more details on how Google handles your data, check out their privacy page.

7. Third-Party Tools

We use a few outside tools to make the site look great and work well (like code libraries and hosting services). When your browser loads these tools, those companies might process basic technical info according to their own privacy rules.

8. Google Fonts

We use Google Fonts to make our text easy to read. When your browser fetches these fonts, Google receives basic technical request data (like your IP address) to deliver the files to your screen.

9. Links to Other Sites

Sometimes we link to other places, like social media, payment services, or documentation. Once you click a link and leave QIFY, you're on their turf. We can't control what they do with your data, so it's always a good idea to skim their privacy policies, too.

10. WhatsApp QR Codes

If you make a WhatsApp QR code, scanning it simply opens the WhatsApp app with a pre-filled message. We don't send the message for you. Once the scanner opens WhatsApp, everything is handled securely by WhatsApp's own terms and policies.

11. UPI QR Codes

Our UPI feature lets you generate a code for your UPI ID and payment amount. QIFY does not process or touch your money. The actual payment happens securely on the scanner's own banking or UPI app.

Always verify the name and amount before sharing a payment code. And please, never type your UPI PIN, passwords, or OTPs into QIFY!

12. WiFi QR Codes

WiFi QR codes are super convenient, but remember: they literally contain your network password in plain text. Anyone who scans it can see your password. We highly recommend only sharing these with people you trust.

13. Keeping Things Safe

We do our very best to build a secure site that collects as little data as possible. But let's be real—nothing on the internet is 100% hacker-proof. Please be smart and avoid entering highly sensitive info online unless it's absolutely necessary.

14. Kids on QIFY

QIFY is made for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect data from children or ask them to make profiles. If you think a child has accidentally shared personal info with us, let us know so we can look into it.

15. You're in Control

You can always choose whether or not to use QIFY. You can block cookies in your browser, and you can manage your ad preferences directly through Google.

16. How Long We Keep Data

Because we don't require an account or save your QR code inputs to a database, there is no personal QR data for us to hold onto! Any basic background technical logs are kept by our hosting or third-party tools according to their own standard deletion schedules.

17. Updates to This Policy

If we add new features or if privacy laws change, we might need to update this page. The "Last updated" date at the top will tell you when we made our latest tweaks. Feel free to check back occasionally.

18. Say Hello

If you have any questions, concerns, or just want to chat about this policy, please reach out! QIFY is proudly operated and developed by Deep Mistry. You can find our contact details on the website.
