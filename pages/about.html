QIFY is an independent technology project created and developed by Deep Mistry. The project focuses on building practical web tools that people can use directly from their browser.

What is QIFY?

QIFY is a collection of browser-based tools built around a simple idea: useful software should be easy to use. The project provides tools for common digital tasks without requiring users to install separate applications for each task.

One of the tools available on QIFY is the QR Code Generator. It supports several types of QR data, including website URLs, plain text, phone numbers, email addresses, contact information, WiFi networks, UPI payment details, and WhatsApp chat links.

The QR generator creates the QR code directly in the user's browser. This means that the information entered into the generator is processed locally by the web application rather than being sent to a QIFY server just to generate the QR code.

Why QIFY was created

Many everyday digital tasks do not need a large application or a complicated account system. A person may only need to turn a website address into a QR code, share WiFi information, prepare a contact QR code, or create a payment QR code.

QIFY is built around these small but useful tasks. The goal is to keep the interface clear, reduce unnecessary steps, and make the tools usable on both desktop and mobile devices.

Browser-based tools

QIFY's QR generator performs QR generation in the browser. For the QR generator, information such as the text, URL, WiFi details, or payment information is used by the page to construct the QR data and generate the result.

This method is useful for information that users may not want to send to a third-party QR generation service. It also allows the generator to work without requiring an account or registration.

Who uses QIFY?

QIFY is designed for a wide range of everyday users. Students can use QR codes to share links and contact information. Shop owners and small businesses can use QR codes for payments, menus, customer communication, and other digital interactions.

Developers and other technical users can also use the tools for quick testing and for creating QR codes without installing additional software.

The tools are also useful in cybercafes and other places where people help customers with online and digital services.

About the QIFY QR Code Generator

The QIFY QR Code Generator currently offers separate options for URL, text, mobile number, email, contact, WiFi, UPI, and WhatsApp QR codes.

A URL QR code can hold a website address, while text QR codes can store regular text. Contact and phone options can be used to share contact information. WiFi QR codes can include network connection details. UPI QR codes can contain payment information, and the WhatsApp option can create a link that opens a WhatsApp conversation with a pre-filled message.

Users can download the QR codes generated by the tool directly from the browser after they are created.
