About QIFY

QIFY is an independent technology project created and developed by Deep Mistry, founder of QIFY.

QIFY focuses on practical browser-based tools, including QR code generation tools for URLs, text, contacts, WiFi, UPI payments, email, phone numbers and WhatsApp.

Contact for Website Issues

If you experience a problem while using a QIFY tool, please describe the problem clearly. Including the name of the tool, the browser you are using and the steps that caused the problem can make it easier to investigate.

Please do not send passwords, UPI PINs, OTPs, authentication codes or other sensitive credentials when reporting an issue.

Feedback and Suggestions

QIFY is continuously developed and improved. Suggestions about usability, new tools, accessibility, performance and other improvements are welcome.

When sending feedback, explain what you would like to see changed and why the change would be useful. This helps us understand the problem and evaluate possible improvements.

Before Contacting QIFY

For QR codes containing payment, WiFi, contact or other personal information, please first verify the information entered into the generator. A QR code contains the information supplied by the user and should be reviewed before it is shared.

For problems involving an external service such as WhatsApp, a UPI application, a browser or another third-party service, the relevant provider may also need to be contacted because QIFY does not control those external services.
